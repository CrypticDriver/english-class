🧠 答题心法（每题必读）

口语高分的根本顺序

1️⃣ 先有观点（Point first）
听到题先想清楚 “我的立场 / 态度是什么”——先亮出一句明确的观点，不要上来就堆句子。

2️⃣ 再造句子（Sentences follow）
观点定了，再用句式 + 词组去 支撑它（举例 / 解释 / 对比）。句子是为观点服务的，不是为了用而用。

一句话：先回答 “What do I think?”，再回答 “How do I say it?”——顺序反了就变成背句子，考官一听就知道。
第 6 节你给自己留的三件事，这节课带进去：
① What do you do / What happened 类问题：先一句结论，再一步一句，数到 12 换句。话题②整个就是 What happened，这节课的主战场。
② 出门前默念：clients（不是 guests）、close（不是 closed）、a few（不是 few）。
③ 预习页词组至少用出来一个。这节课候选：look stupid / started off / turned around。

🤔 为什么选这两题？

老师很可能会追问“你为什么选这两个话题”——提前想好答法，自然不卡壳

I picked these two because they're both about asking. The first one is about why people stop asking. In meetings, in shops, everywhere.

The second one is a story from my own work. A client who was difficult, until we both started asking real questions. So one is the problem, and the other is what happens when you fix it.

中文备用（万一老师用中文问）：这两题都在说"问"。第一题讲人为什么不问了，开会不问、买东西不问。第二题是我自己工作里的事，一个很难搞的客户，到我们俩开始互相问真问题之后才顺起来。一个是问题本身，一个是把它解决之后的样子。

🙋 话题 ① 现在的人是不是越来越不敢问问题了？

Do you think people today are more afraid to ask questions? Afraid to ask in meetings, afraid to ask when shopping, afraid to ask when they don't understand something — why do you think that is? — Society，观点类话题。先表态，再给两个原因，一个例子。

🎯 核心观点（选 2-3 个你最有共鸣的）

  • 先表态：是的，而且我在客户身上天天看到 — 客户听不懂也不问，点头，然后回去用错。这是你第 6 节说的"让客户用自己的话复述"的来源：因为他们不会主动问
  • 原因一：问问题等于承认自己不懂，大家怕显得笨 — 开会里尤其明显，领导在，同事在，问一句就像暴露了自己。第 2 节"客户的笨问题"那题你的立场就是没有笨问题，这里直接接上
  • 原因二：答案太容易查，问人反而显得懒 — 手机就在手边，什么都能搜。于是"我可以自己查"变成了不问的借口，结果查了半天还是没懂
  • 升华：不敢问的代价不是当场尴尬，是后面的误会 — 当场问一句花十秒，不问的话，一个月后发现大家理解的根本不是一回事。做客户经理这几年，几乎所有大麻烦都是没人问那一句造成的

🗣️ 实用句式

句式用法说明
Yes, and I see it every day with my clients.表态 + 立刻拉到自己的工作场景，一句话 9 个词
People are afraid of looking stupid, especially in front of their boss.原因一。look stupid 是"显得笨"，不是 be stupid（真笨）
The answer is one search away, so asking a person feels lazy.原因二。one search away 和第 6 节 one buzz away 是同一个模子
They nod, say "got it," and then use it wrong.具体画面，比"客户不懂"有力。三个短动作连着说
Asking costs you ten seconds. Not asking costs you a month.收尾对比句。两句各 5 个词，停顿要清楚

📝 Part 2 完整范文

Cue card: Do you think people are more afraid to ask questions today? Why?

Yes, I think so. And I see it every day with my clients. I explain a feature, they nod, they say "got it." Then they go back and use it wrong. They understood maybe half. But nobody asked.

I think there are two reasons. The first is simple: people are afraid of looking stupid. In a meeting, the boss is there, the colleagues are there. One question feels like a confession. So you stay quiet and hope someone else asks.

The second reason is newer. The answer is always one search away. So asking a person feels lazy. People think, "I'll look it up later." Then they don't. Or they do, and they still don't get it.

At work, I've learned to work around this. I don't ask "Do you understand?" Everyone says yes to that. I ask them to say it back in their own words. If they can't, we go again. No one feels stupid, because I made it part of the process.

So my view is this. Asking costs you ten seconds. Not asking costs you a month. Almost every big problem I've dealt with started with one question nobody asked.

这篇范文的句子长度：最长 13 词，大部分 5 到 9 词。观点类话题你本来就说得短，这篇就是按你的短句写的。第四段是你第 6 节自己说的"let them repeat and use their words"，这次预习先出声练，上课直接拿出来用。

词组中文意思用法 / 为什么好用

🔄 话题 ② 有没有一开始很难搞、后来却最顺的客户？

Since you've been a client manager, has there been a client who started off really difficult but ended up being your smoothest partnership? What happened in between? — Work/Professional，讲故事类话题。这是你最容易 20 词起、最容易冒 I will 的题型，这节课的主战场。

🎯 核心观点（选 2-3 个你最有共鸣的）

  • 先一句结论，再讲故事 — 开头就说"有，而且现在是我最顺的客户"。然后才是"一开始怎么难、中间发生什么、现在怎样"。结论不先说，考官听三分钟还不知道你要讲到哪
  • 难搞的客户往往不是人难搞，是对接方式错了 — 对面是采购，不是用的人。每次问题都要转三手，到你这已经变味了。他们觉得你答非所问，你觉得他们问得不清楚
  • 转折点是一个具体的动作，不是"慢慢就好了" — 比如：你直接找到他们的工程师，开了一次 30 分钟的会，换了对接人。考官要听的是 what happened，就是这一个动作
  • 升华：最顺的合作不是从没出过问题，是双方都知道怎么问对方 — 这和话题①接上了。现在他们有问题直接发消息问，你也直接问他们"这个功能你们到底拿来干什么"。省掉了中间所有猜

🗣️ 实用句式

句式用法说明
Yes. One client started off as my hardest account. Now they're my easiest.开头结论句。started off 是题目原词，直接拿来用
Every question went through three people before it reached me.一句话说清"难搞在哪"。注意 went，过去时
The turning point was one meeting.6 个词。先把"转折点"亮出来，再展开
I asked for a different point of contact.第 6 节升级句里的 point of contact，这次先练好再上
Since then, we skip the middle. They ask me directly, I ask them directly.收尾。since then 后面接现在时，说的是现在的状态

⏱️ 讲故事的三个时态，提前定好

这题要在三个时间里来回跳，中文全靠"了 / 以前 / 现在"，英文靠动词变形。上场前把每段用哪个时态定死，就不会乱。
讲到哪一段用什么例句
一开始怎么难（过去的状态）一般过去时 / used toThey used to send everything through procurement. Every question went through three people.
中间发生了什么（一次性动作）一般过去时I asked for a meeting with their engineer. We sat down for thirty minutes.
现在怎么样（当前状态）一般现在时Now they message me directly. We skip the middle.
从那以后一直（持续到现在）现在完成时Since then, we haven't had a single misunderstanding.
I will 预警：这题整个在讲过去和现在，没有一句需要 will。你想说"我就会找他们工程师"，那个"会"是"当时做了"，是 asked，不是 will ask。"现在他们有问题会直接找我"，那个"会"是习惯，是 they message me，不是 they will message me。

📝 Part 2 完整范文

Cue card: Describe a client who was difficult at first but became a great partnership. What changed?

Yes, there's one I always think of. They started off as my hardest account. Now they're my easiest. The difference was one meeting.

At the start, everything went through their procurement team. Not the people using the product. So every question went through three people before it reached me. By then it had changed shape. They thought I was dodging their questions. I thought their questions made no sense. Both of us were a bit right.

The turning point was simple. I asked for a different point of contact. I said, "Let me talk to whoever actually uses this." They agreed. We sat down with their engineer for thirty minutes. In that meeting, I finally asked, "What are you trying to do with this feature?" Nobody had asked that before. The answer was not what procurement had told me.

After that, we skipped the middle. The engineer messages me directly. I ask them directly. When I explain something, I ask them to say it back. When they want something, they tell me the real reason, not the polite version.

Since then, we haven't had a single real misunderstanding. They're not our biggest account. But they're the one where I never have to guess. Honestly, nothing about the product changed. We just cut out the people in between.

这篇范文的结构就是你的三件事里的第一件：第一段 4 句结论。第二段过去怎么难。第三段一个动作。第四段现在怎样。第五段收。最长一句 11 词。全文零个 will。

🎓 Part 3 追问 + 参考答案

考官追问参考回答（含高分表达）
Why do you think they were difficult at the beginning?Honestly, they weren't. The setup was. Wrong people were talking to each other.
What did you learn from that experience?When a client feels difficult, I now check who I'm actually talking to first. Usually that's the problem, not the person.
Do you do anything differently with new clients now?Yes. In the first week, I ask for the person who uses the product. Not just the one who pays for it.
Can every difficult client be turned around like that?No. Some people just don't want a partnership. But I'd say most difficult clients are a communication problem, not a personality problem.

🔑 词组精讲

词组中文意思用法 / 为什么好用
start off as一开始是题目原词。started off... ended up... 是一对，开头说前者，结尾说后者
go through sb.经过某人的手描述"转三手"。went through 过去时，别说 go through
change shape变了样 / 变味了比 the message was distorted 口语得多
dodge a question躲问题 / 答非所问客户觉得你在躲，这个词一下就说清了
the turning point转折点讲故事的信号词。说出这四个字，考官就知道重点来了
point of contact对接人第 6 节已经出现过，这节必须说出来一次
sit down with sb.和某人坐下来谈比 have a meeting with 多一层"认真谈"的意思
skip the middle跳过中间环节把"不再转三手"说成一个动作
cut out去掉 / 砍掉cut out the middleman 是固定说法，这里改成 the people in between 更贴你的故事
turn sth. around把局面扭转过来追问里的词。turn a client around = 把难搞客户变顺

💡 为什么能拿高分

  • 结论在第一句 — "started off hardest, now easiest" 四秒钟就把整个故事的框架交给考官，后面怎么讲都不会散
  • 难搞的原因不是骂人 — 说"对接方式错了"而不是"客户很烂"，立刻显得专业，也给后面的转折留了路
  • 转折点是一个动作 — "换对接人，开一次会，问一个问题"。What happened 要的是动作，不是"后来慢慢好了"
  • 三个时态各管一段 — 过去难、过去的那一次、现在顺。时态对了，故事线自然清楚，不用加"first / then / finally"
